NextShelved

2026

I kept trying to find a book I could only describe, not name. I knew the feeling, the pace, the kind of night I wanted to spend with it, and every search box wanted a title I did not have. So I built NextShelved. You say what you're in the mood for, in your own words, and it comes back with real books, each with a short reason it fits. I wanted it to feel like asking a bookseller who actually listened, and then getting a few good answers instead of a pile of almosts. It's live at nextshelved.com.
