Block / Lifecycle Growth & Automation

At Block, I helped improve the infrastructure and execution behind large-scale lifecycle programs. That meant connecting audience strategy, data, orchestration, experimentation, and quality assurance to deliver better customer experiences.

A lending lifecycle refresh reached 83,500 merchants and delivered measurable improvements in engagement and conversion. The work reinforced something I believe: better systems give good strategy room to perform.

SoFi / Lifecycle Operations & Automation

At SoFi, I worked on the operational side of lifecycle marketing, spanning campaign architecture, segmentation, QA, and production processes. One area of focus was using AI-assisted evaluation and reporting to make repetitive quality checks more consistent and actionable.

The goal wasn't automation for its own sake. It was giving teams clearer signals, fewer avoidable errors, and more time for the work that benefits from judgment.

NextShelved / Independent Product

I kept trying to find a book I could describe, but not name. I knew the mood and the kind of story I wanted, yet most search boxes expected a title or genre. So I started building NextShelved.

It's a different approach to discovery: describe what you're in the mood for in your own words, then get real book recommendations with a reason each might fit. A small product built around a very human problem.
