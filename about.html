01 / Work

Where people
meet technology.

I work in marketing technology, with a focus on CRM and customer experiences. I’m interested in how a good idea becomes something useful: the tools behind it, the decisions along the way, and the experience on the other side.

That interest extends to product and to building tools of my own. I like understanding how something works, then finding a clearer way to do it.

02 / Design

Warmth. Character.
A sense of place.

Outside work, I spend a lot of time thinking about interiors, personal style, and everyday objects. I’m drawn to classic shapes, natural materials, and spaces that feel comfortable and lived in.

I like modern technology with a little warmth around it. A good desk, a thoughtful room, and a tool that does its job well all matter to me.

03 / Places

Something else
to notice.

Travel and amateur photography give that curiosity somewhere else to go. Architecture, the atmosphere of a hotel, or a small detail on a walk can be as interesting to me as the main attraction.

This site gives those interests a place alongside the things I build.
