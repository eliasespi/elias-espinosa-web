A little context

I build at the intersection of technology, strategy, and experience. I like making complex things feel straightforward, whether that's a marketing system, an AI-enabled workflow, or a new digital product.

Over the past few years, I've worked across Block, SoFi, and Forbes Advisor, often as the person connecting big ideas to the systems that make them possible.

I studied Information, Communication & Technology at Florida State University. That blend of people, communication, and technology still shapes how I approach problems today.

I'm interested in the entire experience, from the underlying architecture to the details that make something genuinely good to use.
