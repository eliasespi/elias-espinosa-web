About

Hi there.

I’m Elias Espinosa. I work on marketing automation, and on the strategy that should come before the tool is even open.

I learned the craft on Square’s banking lifecycle team: journeys, messages, and the operational detail that keeps a program kind. At Forbes Advisor I worked in CRM, including the survey launch that let readers answer back. I am now a Marketing Technology Solutions Architect at Block.

I live in the Miami–Fort Lauderdale area. Florida State is where I got an early education in showing up for a group. I was a senator in student government, and a morale coordinator for Dance Marathon.

People who have worked with me tend to mention the same two things: I am easy to be around, and I am particular about the details. I want both of those to stay true.
