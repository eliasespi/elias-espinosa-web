01 / Work

Marketing, systems,
and customer experience.

I work in marketing technology and CRM, usually in the space between strategy, systems, and execution. I like figuring out how a customer experience should work, then making the tools and operations behind it hold up.

Product thinking is increasingly part of that for me: clear problems, useful systems, fewer unnecessary steps.

02 / Design

Traditional, warm,
and lived in.

My taste leans traditional rather than trendy: warm woods, tailored shapes, natural materials, and technology that knows when to disappear.

The same instinct shows up in clothes, rooms, and digital products. Structure first, details second, and nothing there just to prove it can be.

03 / Places

Travel, hotels,
and a camera.

I notice places through architecture, hotels, light, signage, and the way a room or street feels. Photography gives me a reason to pay closer attention.

I am less interested in collecting destinations than understanding what gives a place its character.
