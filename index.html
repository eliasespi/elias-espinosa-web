How I work

Three questions before a workflow earns a send.

01

Start with the moment.

Automation is only useful when it meets someone at a real point in the relationship. The tool comes second.

02

Make the logic visible.

Journeys, rules, and handoffs should be something a teammate can audit on an ordinary Tuesday.

03

Keep the measure honest.

A test should describe what the customer did, not only what the platform says it sent.
